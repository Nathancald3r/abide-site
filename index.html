Abide

A free Bible memory, daily devotion and prayer app.

Abide helps you memorize Scripture with spaced repetition (read, type the first letters, or say the verse aloud), keeps a daily reflection journal, and holds a simple prayer list. It is free, with no ads, no accounts, and no in-app charges of any kind.

Where to find it

Abide is an Android app. It is not in an app store yet; it is installed directly on the developer's own device. This page describes the app.

Privacy

  • Everything you write (journal, prayers, verses, settings) stays on your device.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • The app only contacts a Bible text service when you look up a verse, and sends only the passage reference you typed.

Scripture

The app links to David Guzik's commentary at Enduring Word, Blue Letter Bible and YouVersion, and to the daily reading at utmost.org. It does not copy their content.
